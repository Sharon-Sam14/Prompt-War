# Brand Direction — EventCraft AI

## Product Personality (5 adjectives)

1. **Warm** — ivory & aubergine palette, editorial feel, never cold or sci-fi
2. **Elegant** — restrained sophistication, no gradients/glass/neon, premium SaaS standard
3. **Trustworthy** — rules-owned math, visible engine, honest badges, no fake activity
4. **Clarity** — typographic hierarchy, semantic tokens, data visible at a glance
5. **Joyful** — small moments (confetti, count-ups, celebratory moments) without glitter

## Who It Attracts

- **Couples** planning weddings who want guidance without pressure
- **Families** throwing birthdays who need budget + timeline + vendor checklist
- **Community organizers** running festivals, fundraisers, block parties

## Emotional Promise

> "Your celebration, handled."

The app doesn't promise to replace your vendors or friends — it handles the math, timeline, and checklists so you can focus on what matters. The numbers are engine-owned, the tone is warm, and the UI respects your intelligence.

## Visual Idea

**Editorial warmth + product clarity.** Think: a premium wedding planning magazine meets a well-organized SaaS dashboard. The visual system has two gears:

- **Warmth:** ivory backgrounds, wood-grained SVGs, soft duotones, hand-drawn icons, human copy
- **Clarity:** semantic tokens, tight typography, direct labels, progress that's instantly readable

## 3 Things to Avoid

1. **Generic gradients** behind body text or data — if gradients are used, they're soft, tonal, and only on hero/covers/stat highlights with contrast verified
2. **Clone of existing planners** — no fake "drag-and-drop calendar," no generic card decks, no clip-art icons
3. **AI slop aesthetics** — no glassmorphism, no neon glows, no constant animation, no sparkle-overload. "Joyful" means small, tasteful moments, not a light show.

## Current UI — What Makes It Forgettable

- Generic shadcn cards with no imagery or brand touch
- Flat solid colors with no vertical accent variation
- No hero visual, no product preview, no illustrative moments
- Generic feature grid with stock-like descriptions
- No brand moments: no confetti, no count-up celebrations, no cover illustrations
- Microcopy that's functional but not warm or specific
